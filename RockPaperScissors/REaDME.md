A classic Rock Paper Scissors game where you play against the computer. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, featuring real-time score tracking and instant win/lose/draw results.

## ⚙️ How to Play

1. Choose Rock 🪨, Paper 📄, or Scissors ✂️
2. Computer randomly selects its move
3. The winner is decided based on classic rules:
   - Rock beats Scissors
   - Scissors beats Paper
   - Paper beats Rock
4. Score updates automatically after each round
